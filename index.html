About Me

I am a driven student innovator at Ismailia STEM High School in Egypt, passionately trying to save the world, one water molecule at a time, by tackling critical environmental challenges like water scarcity and pollution. Growing up in a rural village in Dakahlia, I witnessed firsthand the urgent need for sustainable solutions, which is a polite way of saying: I couldn’t ignore the problem even if I tried (and I didn’t). That experience continues to fuel my work today, along with a solid plate of makarona bshamil, which may or may not be my true source of innovation.

My journey in innovation has earned recognition at both national and institutional levels. I hold a patent-pending wastewater treatment system filed with the Egyptian Patent Office and have achieved 1st Place at the Cairo International Exhibition for Innovation (2023), 3rd Place at Mansoura University Scientific Exhibition (2022), and Finalist recognition for the State Award for Young Innovator (2021). Most recently, I secured 2nd Place at Tech Innovation ’26 (yes, I’m still thinking about that missing 1st place) and qualified as a Finalist in the Egypt Science and Engineering Fair (ESEF) 2026 at the Republican level, a milestone that proves I’m either very committed or just really stubborn.

Beyond science fairs, I’ve been a multi-year participant in Intel ISEF-affiliated competitions and an invited Exhibitor at the First Maspero Exhibition for Inventors in collaboration with the Egyptian Space Agency. When I’m not busy trying to innovate my way through environmental crises, you’ll probably find me playing paddle like it’s the Olympics or at the gym convincing myself that lifting weights is “research on human energy systems.”

My long-term vision is to develop and scale sustainable environmental solutions that serve communities in Egypt and beyond, ideally while staying well-fed and occasionally winning first place. I’m always open to connecting with fellow researchers, innovators, and organizations committed to environmental sustainability and youth empowerment in STEM (or anyone who has a great makarona bshamil recipe).
